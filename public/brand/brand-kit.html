Brand Voice
✓ We say
  • "An entire SDR team that runs on a $7 server"
  • "We find at least $50K in waste or we walk away"
  • "Your sales call ends. The proposal is in their inbox in 20 minutes."
  • "Production-ready. Not a proof of concept."
  • Concrete numbers. Specific outcomes.
✗ We don't say
  • "Leverage cutting-edge AI solutions"
  • "Transformative digital journey"
  • "Unlock your potential"
  • "Seamless synergy"
  • Jargon without numbers behind it.
Design Rules
Border Radius

Zero. All elements are hard-edged squares and rectangles. The single exception is the reactor knob (circular by function).

Grid System

4 vertical columns at left-6, left-1/3, left-2/3, right-6. Horizontal lines at 25%, 50%, 75%. "+" markers at every intersection.

Aesthetic

Brutalist Signal / Technical Premium. Dark backgrounds, amber accent, geometric grid, noise texture overlay at 3% opacity.
